Shadowstep: Ninja Assassin: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 November 2026

This policy explains how the game Shadowstep: Ninja Assassin ("the app"), published by SUMAR_TECH ("we"), handles information. It covers the app on Android phones and tablets.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. You don't need an account to play, and we don't ask for your name, email or phone number. Your game progress (levels reached, stars, cash, outfits and your settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We run no servers of our own that receive it.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: an occasional ad between levels, and optional ads you can choose to watch to double your cash or to continue a level. The app shows no ads once you own the Shadow Pass. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings, Ad privacy choices. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings, Google, Ads.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase, the Shadow Pass, is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only learns whether your Google account owns it, so it can unlock it on your devices.

Other games

More games lists other games from SUMAR_TECH. Tapping one opens its page in the Google Play Store app; nothing is sent to us.

Content and age

The game contains stylised cartoon violence (ninja strikes and guards who shoot). It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Ad and purchase traffic from the app travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
