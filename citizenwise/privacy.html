CitizenWise: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 November 2026

This policy explains how the Android app CitizenWise ("the app"), published by SUMAR_TECH ("we"), handles information. The app helps people study for the United States naturalization test: the civics questions, the English reading and writing test and mock interviews.

What we collect

Nothing. You don't need an account, and the app never asks for your name, email, phone number or any ID number. Your study progress, settings, and the answers you enter in "My answers" (your state, your representatives and similar) are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. We have no server that receives them.

Speech

The app reads questions aloud using your phone's built-in text-to-speech. The app does not record your voice and does not use the microphone.

Sharing a result

If you tap "Share my score", the app opens your phone's share menu with a short text. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share it with, and that app then handles it under its own terms.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase (Citizen Pass) is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only learns whether your Google account owns it, so it can unlock it on your devices.

Links to official websites

The app links to official pages such as uscis.gov so you can check current test information at the source. Those sites open in your browser and handle your visit under their own terms.

Not a government app

CitizenWise is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) or any government agency. Questions and answers come from the official USCIS civics test lists, which can change; always check uscis.gov/citizenship before your interview. The app is not legal advice.

Children

The app is meant for adults preparing for naturalization and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Purchase traffic travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) between your device and Google Play.

Changes

We may update this policy, for example if we add ads or statistics in a later version. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
