Carried privacy policy

SUMAR_TECH · Last updated 25 November 2026

Carried is a game about a small seed carried across the world on the backs of giants. We built it so that it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Carried does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information or usage data. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no sign-in.

What stays on the device

  • Your progress: which levels are finished and which seeds you have found.
  • Your settings: music, sound, vibration and picture quality.
  • Whether the full journey has been bought, so the game opens without asking Google Play every time.

This information is kept only in the app's private storage on the device. We never see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Purchases

The first part of Carried is free. The rest of the journey is a single one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy; we never receive your payment details. A purchase belongs to your Google account, so it comes back by itself if you reinstall the game or move to a new device.

Children

Carried has no words, no chat and no links out of the game, and it shows no advertising. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to share with third parties and nothing to request or delete from us.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
