Twistwise: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026

This policy explains how the app Twistwise ("the app"), published by SUMAR_TECH ("we"), handles information. It covers the app on Android phones and tablets.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email or phone number. The cubes you enter, your solve times, your best results and your settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. The app does not send them to us.

Camera

The camera is used only if you choose "Scan with camera". The picture is read on your device to find the colour of each sticker. Pictures are not saved, not uploaded and not shared with anyone, and the camera is switched off as soon as you leave the scan screen. You can always enter a cube by hand instead, and you can withdraw the camera permission at any time in Android's settings.

Advertising

The app shows an ad only when you choose to watch one, for an extra solve or a camera scan. There are no ads when the app opens and none during a solve, and no ads at all once you own Twistwise Pro. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device model, operating system, and interactions with ads. Google's use of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Privacy and purchases → Ad privacy options. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase (Twistwise Pro) is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only learns whether your Google account owns it, so it can unlock it on your devices.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Ad and purchase traffic from the app travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
