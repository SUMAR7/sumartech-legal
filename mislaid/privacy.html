Mislaid: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 September 2026

This policy explains how the game Mislaid ("the app"), published by SUMAR_TECH ("we"), handles information. It covers the app on Android phones and tablets.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. You don't need an account to play, and we don't ask for your name, email or phone number. Everything the game remembers — which lane you are in, what you have found, your hints and your settings — is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. The app does not send your progress or your gameplay to us.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and only ones you choose to watch: there is a single optional ad, offered when you have run out of hints and ask for another. There are no ads between lanes, no ads when you open the game, and no banner. You can decline and keep playing; nothing in the game is locked behind watching one.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device model, operating system, and interactions with ads. Google's use of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad preferences. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads.

Purchases

The app offers one optional one-time purchase, the Finder's Pass, which removes ads and gives you the extra hint without one. It is made and paid for entirely through Google Play: we never see or store payment details. The app keeps a note on the device of whether your Google account owns it, so it works offline, and asks Google Play to confirm it when the game starts. There is nothing else to buy — no subscription, no hint packs, and no currency.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no chat, no sharing and no links to social media. A grown-up can find this policy under Settings → Privacy.

Other games

Settings lists our other games. Tapping one opens its page in the Google Play Store app; we do not track who follows those links beyond the install referrer Google Play provides to us.

Security

Ad and purchase traffic from the app travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
