StayValid: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how the Android app StayValid ("the app"), published by SUMAR_TECH ("we"), handles information. The app helps people in the United Arab Emirates keep track of when their documents expire (Emirates ID, residence visa, passport and others) and works out end-of-service gratuity, final settlement, leave, overtime and notice pay under UAE labour law.

What we collect

Nothing. You don't need an account, and the app never asks for your email, phone number or any ID number. What you enter (the names you give family members, which documents they have and their expiry dates, the salary figures and dates you type into the calculators, and your settings such as language) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. If Android's own backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in that backup. We run no servers that receive it, and the app contains no analytics and no advertising.

Reminders

If you turn reminders on, the app schedules notifications on your phone before each document expires. The dates and the reminders stay on your device; nothing is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in your phone's notification settings.

Backup to a file

With StayValid Family you can save your board to a file and restore it later. The file is created on your phone and goes only where you choose to save or send it. It contains the names and dates you entered, so keep it somewhere private.

Sharing a result

If you tap "Share" on a calculator result, the app opens your phone's share menu with the result as text. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share it with, and that app then handles it under its own terms.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase (StayValid Family) is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only learns whether your Google account owns it, so it can unlock it on your devices.

Links to official websites

The app links to official government pages (for example ICP, GDRFA, RTA and MOHRE) so you can renew or check details at the source. Those sites open in your browser and handle your visit under their own terms.

Not a government app

StayValid is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the UAE government, MOHRE, ICP, GDRFA, RTA or any other authority. Calculations are a guide based on the published law (Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 and its executive regulations, and the DIFC and ADGM employment laws) and are not legal advice.

Children

The app is meant for adults managing their household's documents and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Purchase traffic travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) between your device and Google Play.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
