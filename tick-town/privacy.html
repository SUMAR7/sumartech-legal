Tick Town privacy policy

SUMAR_TECH · Last updated 14 September 2026

Tick Town is a game for children that teaches telling the time. We built it so that it collects nothing at all.

What we collect

Nothing. Tick Town does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information or usage data. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no sign-in. The Android app does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send any data anywhere.

What stays on the device

  • Each child's profile: an animal, an age, a house colour and, if a grown-up adds one, a first name.
  • Progress in the game: which time-telling skills they have practised, the photo album of in-game moments, and house decorations.
  • The "My Day" routine a grown-up enters (for example "bath at 7:00").
  • Settings such as sound, music and clock wording.

This information is kept only in the app's private storage on the device. We never see it. A grown-up can delete a child's profile in the grown-ups' corner, and uninstalling the app deletes everything.

Spoken instructions

Tick Town reads its instructions aloud using the text-to-speech engine that is built into the device. The game only sends it the game's own sentences (such as "Get Mia to the bus at half past 8"), never anything about your child.

Children

Tick Town is designed for children and follows Google Play's Families policy. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to share with third parties and nothing to request or delete from us.

Purchases

Tick Town is a one-time purchase on Google Play. There is nothing to buy inside the game. Google handles the purchase under its own privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
